## Findings that change the program

1. The complete dialogue is the primary source; the earlier 118-node graph inventory is secondary context.
2. Existing signaling can be solved by copying its public answer table. Existing partial-visibility deception can ask for hidden labels without identifying evidence. These do not establish the stronger capability claims in the prior roadmap.
3. Existing T1 preserves matched facts and separate endpoints, but its single-answer contract cannot by itself establish inquiry regret or recovery. The new diagnostic engine makes observations contingent on chosen tests.
4. Late dialogue claims of shipped code and pilot results conflict with the missing reproducibility artifacts and a later offer to write the pilot. Neither version of the quoted numerical results is adopted as measured evidence.
5. Several methodological statements in the log need further correction: CI overlap is not equivalence, a zero average regret gap is not action invariance, an optimum-based loss gap cannot establish negative damage, and a zero oracle gap alone does not prove matching.

## Known weaknesses and outstanding hypotheses

The games have four latent states and bounded depth. The diagnostic partition matrix is supplied. The source model class is supplied through explicit noise and payoff assumptions. Enumeration is a valid solver, not a defeated shortcut. Parameter variation does not establish generalization to new causal rules, game structures, or languages.

The source has a scripted, payoff-sensitive response rather than a learned adaptive policy. Blind trials stipulate a sender audit belief and no observation of contract settlement. This is a controlled information intervention, not a model of every real evaluation protocol. Certificate withholding captures one truthful misleading mechanism; unrestricted selective disclosure, source disagreements under different priors, and arbitrary framing remain distinct future experiments.

The presenter is constrained to permutations. Its positive-control policy assumes a first-row investigator. Accurate/shuffled/absent opponent-profile experiments and learned two-provider trials are specified but have not been conducted. Fixed-schedule mediation analysis and paired statistical aggregation are likewise future work; existing T1 pairing concepts should be reused with the new metric contract rather than silently feeding it incompatible records.

The optional quality reveal supports a recovery contrast, but it does not require an intermediate forecast. A paired-clone design is needed to measure before/after change without altering the default interaction through belief elicitation.

All runtime gates remain open. No claims about internal update-rule modification, broad strategic intelligence, deception in real people, general recursive reasoning, or mathematical novelty follow from source implementation.

## Highest-value continuation

First obtain isolated regression and independent-oracle evidence. Then add explicit truthful-subset selection probabilities and sincere-disagreement controls to the strategic family. The next independent family should use observationally equivalent causal mechanisms with different intervention responses, followed by held-out rule and representation transfer tied to the recursive-gluing sibling's stabilized evidence contract.
